Privacy Policy

All 4 You Field Ops app · Last updated November 11, 2026

All 4 You Field Ops ("the app") is the work app All 4 You Electrical ("we", "us") uses to schedule jobs, track time, and prepare estimates and invoices. It is for our employees. Accounts are created by our office; the public cannot sign up.

What the app collects

  • Employee account details: name, work email, phone, role, and the hours you clock.
  • Location: only at the moment you clock in or out, take a job photo, or record a customer sign-off, and only with your permission. The app does not track location in the background.
  • Photos: only photos you choose to take or attach to a job, with your permission to use the camera or photo library.
  • Customer and job records: customer names, addresses, contact details, job notes, estimates, invoices and signatures entered while doing the work.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, an address for your phone or browser from Apple, Google or your browser's push service, so we can tell you when you're put on a job or a visit changes. Turning notifications off removes it.
  • Sign-in records: a session token on your device, and sign-in attempts on our server for security.

How it is used

Only to run our business: scheduling and dispatch, payroll and job costing, sending estimates, invoices and appointment confirmations to customers, and keeping job records. We do not sell personal information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers in the app.

Where it is kept and who sees it

Data is stored on our own server, reached over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, with off-site backups. Inside the company, what each person can see depends on their role. We share data only with services that carry out our work for us — for example, the email service that delivers customer emails, and Google Maps or the US Census address service when you look up an address — or when the law requires it. The app's text font is loaded from Google Fonts, which sees your device's IP address.

How long it is kept

Job, time and billing records are kept as long as we need them for the business and as tax and contractor-licensing rules require. When an employee leaves, their sign-in is turned off; their past time and job records stay with the company records.

Your choices

  • You can turn off camera, photo or location access for the app at any time in your phone's Settings.
  • To see, correct or delete your personal information, or to close your account, contact us below.

Children

The app is for our employees and is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

All 4 You Electrical — use the email or phone number on our website, or ask the office.
